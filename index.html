Mission

Zachary is attempting 8,000 strict chin-ups in 24 hours to raise funds for veterans and families served by Wounded Warrior Project®.

All donations are processed through the official external campaign page via the Donate & Livestream link above.

This effort is independent and not endorsed by Wounded Warrior Project or Guinness World Records. Trademarks belong to their respective owners.

The Standard (Non-Negotiable Rep Standard)

  • Supinated chin-ups on a straight bar.
  • Full lockout or near dead-hang on every rep.
  • Chin clearly over the bar each rep.
  • No kipping or lower-body momentum.

Verification

Default plan

Independent verification with continuous-ish video, plus a two-person count log that cross-checks totals throughout the 24-hour window.

Guinness track (parallel)

Guinness World Records requirements will be confirmed in writing if that track is pursued. Independent verification continues regardless.

Guinness Track

Pursuing Guinness World Records category for most chin-ups in 24 hours (palms facing you).

Documentation checklist (draft, not official rules)

  • Confirmed category requirements in writing from Guinness World Records.
  • Two independent witnesses with clear time logs.
  • Continuous video coverage with time markers.
  • Rep standard documentation + rest breaks logged.
  • Signed verification packet submitted post-event.

Safety First

Hard-stop criteria — stop immediately if any of the following occur:

  • Dark/cola urine.
  • Severe swelling.
  • Profound weakness.
  • Severe pain with systemic symptoms.
  • Confusion.
  • Inability to urinate.
  • Dehydration symptoms not improving with fluids/sodium.

Hydration & electrolytes

500–750 mL/hour fluids + 300–600 mg sodium/hour minimum (up to ~1,000 mg/hour in heavy sweat/heat).

Medication & stimulant rule

Avoid NSAIDs during extreme efforts; caffeine only strategic (no panic dosing).

Not medical advice. Health decisions should be made with qualified medical professionals.
